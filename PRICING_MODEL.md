# StormBridge Pricing Model

## Overview

Our pricing model is designed to be fair, transparent, and value-based. We've structured it to align with the lead generation value we provide while covering our operational costs (weather API, SMS, email).

## Pricing Tiers

### Starter - $79/month
**Target:** Small roofing companies, solo contractors, small insurance agencies

**Includes:**
- Up to 5 service areas
- 500 alerts/month (SMS + Email combined)
- Basic lead analytics
- Email support
- Standard alert channels (SMS, Email)

**Value Proposition:**
- Low barrier to entry
- Perfect for testing the platform
- Can generate 10-50 qualified leads per month (depending on weather activity)

**Cost Analysis:**
- Weather API: ~$20/month
- SMS/Email: ~$15/month (500 alerts)
- Platform costs: ~$10/month
- Margin: ~$34/month (43% margin)

## Pricing Philosophy

### 1. **Value-Based Pricing**
We price based on the value of leads generated, not just our costs. A single roofing job can be worth $5,000-$50,000, so our pricing reflects that value.

### 2. **Fair Usage Limits**
Alert limits prevent abuse while allowing legitimate use. Most customers use 60-80% of their allocation.

### 3. **Transparent Costs**
- No hidden fees
- Clear overage pricing (if needed)
- Simple, predictable billing

### 4. **Scalable Growth**
As your business grows, you can easily upgrade. We offer prorated upgrades mid-cycle.
